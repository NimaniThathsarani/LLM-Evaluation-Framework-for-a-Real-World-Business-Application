# ShopEase Customer Support Knowledge Base

This fictional policy document is the source of truth for the evaluation project.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days. Free standard delivery applies to orders of LKR 10,000 or more; otherwise standard delivery costs LKR 500. Express delivery costs LKR 1,200.

## Tracking
Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking link sent by email. If an order has not shipped, a tracking number may not yet exist. The assistant must not invent tracking numbers, courier names, locations, or delivery dates.

## Cancellation
An order may be cancelled only before it has been shipped. Once shipped, the customer may use the return process after delivery if eligible.

## Returns
Eligible items may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original condition, and include original packaging. Final-sale items, personalized items, opened hygiene products, and digital gift cards are non-returnable.

## Refunds
After an approved return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item was damaged, defective, or incorrect.

## Damaged
For a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery and provide the order number plus clear photos. Eligible cases may receive a replacement or refund, including applicable shipping charges.

## Payments
Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available only for orders up to LKR 25,000. The company does not accept cryptocurrency or bank transfer.

## PaymentSecurity
The assistant must never request or expose passwords, OTPs, CVV codes, full card numbers, or other authentication secrets. For payment failures, it may suggest checking entered details, trying again, using another accepted method, or contacting the card issuer/support.

## Accounts
Customers may reset a forgotten password using the 'Forgot Password' option. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. Email address changes require identity verification through customer support.

## Privacy
The assistant must not disclose another customer's personal information, order details, address, phone number, payment data, or account information. It must not claim access to private account/order data unless such data is actually supplied by an authorized system.

## Products
Product availability can change. The assistant should not promise future stock dates unless verified. Product-specific specifications must not be invented when they are not present in the knowledge base.

## Support
Human support is available Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM-6:00 PM Sri Lanka time. The chatbot may recommend human support when the issue requires account verification, private order lookup, or an exception to policy.

## Scope
The chatbot supports questions about this store's products, orders, delivery, returns, refunds, payments, and accounts. It should decline unrelated requests such as homework completion, hacking, or requests for confidential data.
